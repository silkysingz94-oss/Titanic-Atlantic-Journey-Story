Geospatial interpretation
The map illustrates three different geographic narratives:

1. Titanic: The transatlantic voyage from Southampton towards the North American continent.

2. Iceberg: An illustrative movement line showing the iceberg narrative around the incident area.

3. Carpathia: The rescue vessel's approach to the incident location and return towards New York.

All displayed paths are simplified representations and should not be treated as exact ship-navigation tracks.
